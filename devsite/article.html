How Static Sites Fool Scrapers

By Ada Byte — September 1, 2026

Main-content extraction has to tell an article apart from its own chrome. This paragraph is content; the nav above and footer below are boilerplate.

Why boilerplate matters

Every wasted token on repeated navigation is a token not spent on the text the user asked for. Cleaners that strip chrome pay for themselves on the very first crawl of a real site.

Good extraction is mostly good deletion.

The rest of this page exists so markdown output, metadata (title, author, published time), and only-main-content mode all have something honest to chew on.
